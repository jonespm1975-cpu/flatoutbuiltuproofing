14-day cancellation period

Consumers purchasing digital content online generally have a 14-day right to cancel from the day after the contract is entered into. Where digital supply begins during that period, the right may be lost only if the legal conditions for express prior consent, acknowledgement and contract confirmation are satisfied. Do not assume that merely accessing a link removes cancellation rights.

Immediate access consent — implementation required

Before immediate delivery, use an unticked affirmative control stating: “I expressly consent to immediate supply of this digital content and acknowledge that I will lose my 14-day cancellation right once supply begins.” Record consent and provide the required confirmation on a durable medium.
