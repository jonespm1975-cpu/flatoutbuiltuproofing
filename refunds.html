Your 14-day cancellation right

If you are a consumer, you can generally cancel an online digital-content contract within 14 days from the day the contract is made. To cancel, send us a clear statement before that period ends. You do not have to use the optional form below.

If you ask us to begin supplying digital content during the 14-day period, the right to cancel is lost only once supply begins after you have given prior express consent and acknowledged that you will lose that right when supply begins, and we have provided the required contract confirmation on a durable medium that confirms both your consent and acknowledgement. A pre-ticked box, silence, or simply opening a page is not consent.

If either consent or acknowledgement is absent, or the required confirmation has not been provided, we will not rely on the loss of the cancellation right. If you prefer to keep the 14-day cancellation right, contact us about delayed access before ordering.
