Your 14-day cancellation right

If you are a consumer, you can generally cancel an online digital-content contract within 14 days from the day the contract is made. To cancel, send us a clear statement before that period ends. You do not have to use the optional form below.

If you ask us to start supplying digital content during the 14-day period, your cancellation right is lost only when supply begins after you have first given express consent to immediate supply and acknowledged that you will lose the right once supply begins, and we have given you the required confirmation on a durable medium. We will not treat a pre-ticked box, silence, or simply opening a page as that consent.

If any required consent, acknowledgement or confirmation is missing, you retain the applicable cancellation right and will not be charged for digital content supplied during the cancellation period where the law provides that protection. This policy does not claim that the current site has enabled paid checkout or the immediate-supply consent flow.
