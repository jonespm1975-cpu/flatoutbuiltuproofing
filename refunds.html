Digital Course Cancellation and Refunds — DRAFT

NOT FOR PUBLICATION WITHOUT UK LEGAL REVIEW AND BUSINESS DETAILS.

Customers should be shown the course price, access details, trader identity and cancellation terms before paying.

UK distance-selling rules generally provide a 14-day cancellation period for consumers, with a digital-content exception only where all required conditions are met.

Obtain express consent and acknowledgement before immediate digital delivery if relying on the exception, and send the required confirmation.

Explain how to request a refund at [contact email], how complaints are handled, and preserve statutory remedies for faulty or misdescribed content.
