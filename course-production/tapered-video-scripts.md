# Tapered Insulation Masterclass — Video Production Plan
Status: production scripts; not filmed or AI-rendered. UK training only. All visuals require technical review against a named manufacturer's approved system and project drawings.

## Video 01 — Survey, levels and drainage (4–6 minutes)
Visual: roof plan, measured levels, outlet and threshold callouts. Narration: "Before you open a pack of tapered boards, understand the existing deck. Check surveyed levels, drainage positions, deck condition, loading and weather exposure. A roof that looks level may not be level." Animation: roof plan gains high-point/low-point markers, arrows converge on outlets. Pause card: "Do not invent a falls design on site."

## Video 02 — Read the tapered board schedule (5–7 minutes)
Visual: approved manufacturer drawing with fictitious board IDs clearly marked EXAMPLE ONLY. Narration: "Each board code relates to a thickness and orientation. Confirm the datum, board type, thickness transition, direction arrow and sequence against the approved schedule." Animation: colour-coded boards move from numbered delivery stacks to corresponding plan zones. Show one reversed board creating a local high spot, then reset. Do not imply universal board codes.

## Video 03 — Set-out and installation sequence (6–8 minutes)
Visual: dry set-out on a prepared roof, checks against VCL and substrate specification. Narration: "Follow the manufacturer's start point. Dry-check board codes and orientation. Use only the fixing or adhesive method specified for this deck, insulation and wind-uplift design." Animation: boards placed sequentially, joint lines highlighted. Safety overlay: no direct flame to combustible insulation, no work at unprotected edges.

## Video 04 — Outlets, sumps and complex details (5–7 minutes)
Visual: conceptual valley, sump, parapet and threshold sections. Narration: "A good roof needs continuous drainage to a suitable outlet. Check sump geometry, upstand heights, secondary drainage and all transitions against the approved details." Animation: simulated water flow exposes a reverse-fall error. Label: SIMULATION, NOT HYDRAULIC DESIGN.

## Video 05 — Quality control and handover (4–6 minutes)
Visual: inspection checklist, photographs, annotated deviations, outlet and board schedule. Narration: "Check the installed system before it is covered. Record product types, board sequence, fixings, transitions and outlet continuity. Any departure from the design must be reviewed and approved." Animation: pass/fail inspection markers and handover pack.

## Production and publishing rules
- Use original or properly licensed footage and diagrams; get permission for supplier logos and manufacturer drawings.
- Clearly label AI-generated scenes as simulated; never depict them as real site evidence.
- Captions, transcript, UK English narration, accessible controls and mobile portrait crops.
- Have a competent roofing designer or manufacturer technical representative review every lesson.
- Do not activate paid access until the actual videos are delivered behind authentication and Stripe/Supabase entitlements are tested.
- Check current HSE guidance, relevant UK regulations, fire performance and BS 6229 requirements for each real project.
