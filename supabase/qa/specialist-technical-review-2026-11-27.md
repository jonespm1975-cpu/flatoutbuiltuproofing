# Specialist course technical desk review — 27 November 2026

Scope: actual deployed private catalogue in specialist-course-content v5; all 24 entries, 96 lesson sections, 72 multiple-choice questions. This is a desk review and release-gap assessment, not independent competent-person sign-off or a claim of compliance with paid standards. No course content copied into the public repository.

## Findings

Each course has 4 sections (including a case study), 3 questions, 88–148 words of section text, the same exercise and the same six references. All 72 answer indices are in range. No course is approved. The current written material is awareness-level; it is not ready to sell as high-depth specialist training. Correct-answer indexing is a structural check, not validation of assessment difficulty.

## Cross-catalogue release criteria

- Add original, course-specific learning explanations, annotated diagrams and worked examples, meaningful scenario questions with answer rationales, a distinct exercise and a reviewer-approved learning-time estimate. A suggested target is 30 minutes per course; verify by learner trials, not word count alone.
- Clarify that all roof surfaces are treated as fragile until a competent person confirms otherwise; avoid work at height where practicable and prefer collective protection, with suitable rescue arrangements. No learner should undertake roof work on the strength of this online course alone.
- Add chemical, dust, asbestos and hot-work hazard awareness where relevant; use actual product SDS/TDS and approved site-specific control measures, not universal installation figures.
- Separate England's Approved Documents from devolved building requirements; HSE guidance applies in Great Britain, with HSENI and Northern Ireland requirements separately reviewed. Confirm applicable standards and editions with access to the full texts and manufacturer documentation before approval.
- Confirm rights to illustrations/media; use original or properly licensed assets.
- Competent roofing reviewer signs off each revised course; independent consumer contract review remains separate. Verify authenticated paid access and Postmark delivery before production release. Retain all existing release gates.
