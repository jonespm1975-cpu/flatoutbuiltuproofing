01 · Work at height

Plan to prevent falls

Apply the hierarchy of avoiding work at height where practicable, preventing falls and minimising consequences. Consider collective protection before personal fall protection.

  • Check safe access, edge protection and fragile surfaces.
  • Confirm rescue planning before relying on fall-arrest equipment.
  • Keep openings and unprotected edges controlled.
02 · Torch-on fire safety

Control ignition hazards

Assess combustible substrates, concealed voids, nearby materials, gas equipment and weather. Use the approved system method and site hot-work controls.

  • Check whether flame-free methods are required.
  • Use suitable extinguishers and fire-watch arrangements.
  • Never direct flame onto combustible insulation.
03 · RAMS

Risk assessment and method

Identify hazards, who may be harmed, controls, residual risks, responsibilities and stop-work triggers. Review controls when site conditions change.

  • Deliver briefings and record understanding.
  • Coordinate with other contractors and building occupants.
  • Plan materials handling and weather contingencies.
04 · Access

Ladders, scaffolds and MEWPs

Select access based on the duration, task, environment and risks. Check inspection, competence, loading and rescue arrangements.

05 · PPE and tools

Choose and inspect equipment

Consider task-appropriate head, eye, hand, foot and respiratory protection, with fit, compatibility and maintenance checks.

Read tool safety modules
06 · Emergency response

Prepare before work begins

Confirm first aid, emergency communication, access for responders, rescue from height, gas isolation and fire evacuation arrangements.

Site safety briefing checklist

Use these prompts in a competent supervisor-led briefing: What are today's hazards? Who controls access and edges? What is the weather forecast? Are gas and electrical equipment inspected? What are the stop-work conditions? Who raises the alarm and how is rescue arranged?
