Education only

Free material provides general awareness, not a site-specific design, manufacturer specification, professional advice, competence certification, practical torch instruction or permission to carry out hazardous work. High-risk activities

Working at height, hot works, electrical equipment and structural roof work can cause severe injury, death, property damage or fire. Assess each task and use trained, competent people with appropriate safety arrangements. Stop work if controls are missing.
