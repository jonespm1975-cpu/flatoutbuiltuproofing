Questions to explore

What should I check before working on an existing roof?

Confirm safe access, roof structure, fragile surfaces, condition, drainage, existing materials, asbestos considerations where relevant and the assessed method of work. Refer uncertainties to competent people.

Why are upstands and outlets important?

They are common junctions where poor design or workmanship can allow water ingress. They need a system-specific design and inspection.

Can a beginner learn torch-on roofing from this site alone?

No. These notes support learning but do not replace supervised practical training, competency assessment and site-specific controls.
