Cookie and Browser Storage Notice

Last updated: 14 November 2026

The public website code does not currently include analytics or advertising trackers. The sign-in feature uses browser storage to keep the Supabase authentication session so that account and member pages work. This storage is used only for the account service requested by the user. Signing out removes the active session; clearing site data in your browser also removes locally stored session information.

The sign-in page loads the Supabase JavaScript library from jsDelivr. The providers operating sign-in, hosting or payment services may use their own strictly necessary technologies under their published notices. If a Stripe checkout page is presented, Stripe's own cookie and privacy information applies there.

If we introduce analytics, personalised advertising or another non-essential storage or access technology, we will update this notice and obtain any consent required before it is used. See the Privacy Notice for other information about personal data.
