Cookie and Browser Storage Notice

Last updated: 19 November 2026

The sign-in feature uses browser storage for your Supabase authentication session. Separately, optional first-party site analytics run only after you choose “Allow optional analytics”. They count page views and course-link clicks with broad device and QR/search/social attribution. If allowed, a random browser-tab session ID and coarse attribution are kept in sessionStorage; a yes/no consent choice is kept in localStorage so we can honour your preference. No advertising trackers are installed by this site.

The sign-in page loads the Supabase JavaScript library from jsDelivr. The providers operating sign-in, hosting or payment services may use their own strictly necessary technologies under their published notices. If a Stripe checkout page is presented, Stripe's own cookie and privacy information applies there.

You can reject or withdraw optional analytics without losing access to free lessons. Click below to choose again. Turning analytics off stops new tracking and clears the site analytics session values from this browser tab. Analytics events are automatically deleted after 90 days. See the Privacy Notice for further details.
