Information we use, why, and our lawful bases

  • Account and sign-in data: email address, account identifier and authentication/session information, to create and secure an account, confirm an email address, reset a password and provide member pages. The basis is taking steps at your request and performing the account service contract; security checks are also supported by our legitimate interests in protecting the service.
  • Orders, access and consent records: if you buy a course, we process the course identifier, Stripe checkout/payment reference, amount and currency, account identifier, purchase status and any recorded digital-supply consent. We use these details to verify payment, provide course access, send the order confirmation, handle support or refunds and keep required business records. The bases are performing the purchase contract, complying with legal record-keeping duties, and our legitimate interests in preventing fraud and resolving disputes.
  • Enquiries and support: the contact details and message you choose to send, so we can answer you. The basis is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries, or steps towards a contract if the enquiry is about a purchase.
  • Security and technical information: limited service, authentication and security information generated when the website and its providers operate, to keep accounts and the site secure and investigate faults. The basis is our legitimate interests in security and reliable operation, balanced against your rights.
  • Optional first-party analytics (with your consent): visited page, a random browser-tab session identifier, broad device type, course-link clicks and broad source attribution (such as QR, search or social plus a short campaign code). No full referring URLs, IP addresses, email addresses or payment identifiers are added to the analytics events database. Consent is optional and can be withdrawn from the Cookie and Browser Storage Notice. Anonymous page analytics are not combined with named account or payment records; the owner sees separate aggregate account and verified live purchase counts.

We do not currently use this site to send marketing campaigns or to run personalised advertising tracking. We do not make decisions about you solely by automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

Roofing Connect enquiries: The enquiry form prepares a message in your own email app and does not submit or store the form on our website. If you choose to send it, the owner receives the email address and project information you include via Gmail, to review and respond to your enquiry (our legitimate interest in responding to requested enquiries). We will seek your permission before sharing your details with any named participating roofing business. No automatic referral or marketing list is enabled. Enquiry correspondence follows the support-correspondence retention period below.

What happens if you do not provide information?

You can read public pages without creating an account. An email address and authentication details are needed to create or recover an account; without them, you cannot use account-only features. To buy a lesson, you need to provide the information required by checkout, including the UK address Stripe uses to check eligibility. You are not required by law to browse the website or create an account.

How long we keep information

  • Account data: while your account remains open. If you ask us to close it, we will remove or de-identify account data and access records within 30 days where we can. We may retain the minimum transaction, consent or security information needed for legal obligations, fraud prevention or a dispute.
  • Business and transaction records: for at least five years after the 31 January Self Assessment filing deadline for the tax year they relate to, as required for self-employed business records, and longer if an investigation, claim or other legal requirement is still open.
  • Support correspondence: normally for 12 months after the enquiry is resolved, or longer while a complaint, claim or legal matter remains open.
  • Analytics events: deleted automatically after 90 days by a daily database retention job. Opting out prevents further analytics collection; existing events cannot be linked to a named account by this site.
  • Provider security and delivery logs: for the period needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the relevant service, according to the provider's retention settings and terms.

We review information and delete or anonymise it when the applicable purpose and retention period end.

Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your information, restriction of processing, or a copy for portability. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests; we will stop unless we have compelling grounds or need the information for legal claims. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time; this does not affect processing already carried out. Contact flatoutbuiltuproofing@gmail.com to make a request.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). We would appreciate the chance to address your concern first.
