New specialist course · Planned

Tapered insulation & laying boards to falls

Structured training for built-up felt roofing, including how tapered insulation systems direct rainwater towards correctly designed outlets.

1. Reading a tapered layout

Identify high points, low points, board codes, design falls and drainage routes.

2. Preparation & setting out

Check substrates, vapour-control requirements, fixing specifications and approved drawings.

3. Board installation principles

Board sequencing, staggered joints, perimeter details, sumps and avoiding reverse falls.

4. Quality & drainage checks

Check interfaces, outlets, surface continuity and conformity with the designed specification.

5. Built-up felt compatibility

Suitable overlays, membrane systems, manufacturer requirements and fire-safe working methods.

6. Assessment & revision

Original diagrams, examples, common errors and knowledge checks are planned for the complete course.

Proposed price: £9.99 for the specialist course. This new course is not available to purchase yet. Technical instruction must be checked against the specific manufacturer's design and project specification.
