SAFETY FIRST

Good training starts with safe planning

Working at height

Plan access, edge protection and fall prevention before work starts. Use current HSE guidance and site-specific assessments.

HSE working at height guidance ↗

Hot works and fire

Torch-on work can cause serious fires. Follow site hot-work controls, fire-watch requirements and the roofing system manufacturer's instructions.
